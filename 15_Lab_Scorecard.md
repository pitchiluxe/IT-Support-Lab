# Personal Lab Scorecard

Score each completed lab from 0–3:
- 0 = Not attempted
- 1 = Completed with significant guidance
- 2 = Completed independently
- 3 = Completed independently and documented professionally

Track:
- Technical execution
- Troubleshooting methodology
- Documentation
- Customer communication
- Validation
- Escalation judgment
- Security awareness
- Time management

Do not use the score as proof of job qualification. It is only a personal practice tracker.
