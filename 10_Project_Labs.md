## Lab 41 — Vendor Management
Scenario: A classroom technology vendor needs to troubleshoot a recurring issue.

Practice:
- Gather logs
- Reproduce issue
- Open vendor case
- Provide exact technical details
- Track SLA
- Coordinate access
- Test vendor fix
- Close only after validation
