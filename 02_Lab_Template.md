# Standard Lab Template

## Scenario
Describe the fictional school/business environment and the user problem.

## User
Faculty / Staff / Student / Parent / Administrator

## Intake Channel
Phone / Email / In-person / Video / Ticket portal

## Business Impact
Who is affected? Is teaching, administration, communication, or safety affected?

## Objective
What must be restored or accomplished?

## Tools
List the systems, devices and commands you are allowed to use.

## Workflow
1. Receive and document the request.
2. Confirm user identity and affected device/service.
3. Ask structured diagnostic questions.
4. Reproduce or observe the problem.
5. Collect evidence.
6. Form a hypothesis.
7. Test the hypothesis.
8. Implement the least-risk solution.
9. Validate the result.
10. Communicate resolution to the user.
11. Document the ticket.
12. Determine whether a knowledge-base article, escalation, problem record or project change is required.

## Evidence
Record screenshots, logs, error messages, timestamps and configuration changes.

## Validation
Define objective tests proving the service works.

## Customer Follow-Up
Explain what was fixed and what the user should do next.

## Escalation
If unresolved, document:
- What was tested
- Results
- Logs/evidence
- Business impact
- Exact assistance required

## Deliverables
- Completed ticket
- Troubleshooting notes
- Resolution
- Validation evidence
- User-facing response
- Knowledge-base entry when appropriate
