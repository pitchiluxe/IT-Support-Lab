# Service Desk Labs

## Lab 01 — Faculty Laptop Cannot Connect to Wi-Fi
Scenario: A teacher arrives before class and reports that their laptop cannot access the school Wi-Fi.

Practice:
- Intake by phone/in-person
- Identity and device verification
- Determine scope
- Check Wi-Fi status, IP configuration, DNS and authentication
- Test with another network/device
- Restore connectivity
- Document the ticket
- Perform post-resolution follow-up

Success criteria:
- You isolate whether the issue is device, credentials, Wi-Fi, DHCP/DNS or broader outage.
- You document evidence and user impact.

## Lab 02 — Urgent Classroom Outage
Scenario: A classroom projector and teacher computer fail 10 minutes before class.

Practice:
- Prioritize based on instructional impact
- Dispatch support
- Establish workaround
- Restore service
- Document SLA timing
- Follow up with teacher

## Lab 03 — Parent Technology Support
Scenario: A parent reports difficulty accessing a school portal from home.

Practice:
- Professional communication
- Verify account information without exposing sensitive data
- Determine whether issue is account, browser, network or application
- Escalate to application owner when required

## Lab 04 — Complex Escalation
Scenario: Multiple users report the same authentication error.

Practice:
- Correlate tickets
- Recognize possible systemic incident
- Notify appropriate escalation group
- Create incident notes
- Communicate status
- Avoid repeatedly troubleshooting individual users when a shared service is failing

## Lab 05 — Ticket Queue Management
Create 20 fictional tickets with different priorities. Practice:
- Categorization
- Assignment
- SLA tracking
- Escalation
- Status updates
- Closure codes
- Reporting
