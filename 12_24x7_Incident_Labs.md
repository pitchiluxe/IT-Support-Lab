# 24/7 Incident and Operations Labs

## Lab 45 — Major Incident Simulation
At 6:30 AM, before school starts, multiple classrooms lose network connectivity.

Your team must:
- Declare/record incident
- Determine scope
- Assign roles
- Gather evidence
- Establish communications
- Identify workaround
- Escalate
- Restore service
- Validate representative classrooms
- Send closure communication
- Conduct post-incident review

## Lab 46 — Security-Sensitive Support Request
A user reports an unexpected login notification.

Practice:
- Avoid exposing sensitive information
- Follow the organization's incident process
- Preserve evidence
- Escalate to the appropriate security personnel
- Document actions without speculating

## Lab 47 — Executive/CIO Escalation
A senior administrator reports a critical technology problem immediately before an important event.

Practice:
- Remain professional
- Prioritize by business impact
- Communicate concise status updates
- Coordinate technical resources
- Provide workaround
- Confirm resolution

## Lab 48 — Simulated After-Hours Pager
Create five incidents that arrive outside normal hours. Practice:
- Alert acknowledgement
- Initial triage
- Severity classification
- Escalation
- Status updates
- Handoff to day team
